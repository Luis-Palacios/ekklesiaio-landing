Waitlist form states

One component, six states. The same states apply to the closing “Notify me” form on navy.

1 · Default
2 · Focused
3 · Submitting
4 · Invalid email
Enter a valid email address, like you@yourchurch.org.
5 · Success / already joined
You’re on the list. Check your inbox to confirm your email—we’ll let you know as soon as ekklesiaio is ready.
Shown for a new signup and for an email already on the list, so the form never reveals who has signed up.
6 · Server error
Something went wrong on our end. Please try again in a moment.
